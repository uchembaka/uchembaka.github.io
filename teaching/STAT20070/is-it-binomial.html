Section 2 · Lecture 06 · Binomial Distribution

Is It Binomial?

For each scenario, check which of the four conditions actually hold, rather than simply answering yes or no.

A situation is Binomial only when all four hold

01A fixed number of trials, n
02Only two possible outcomes per trial (success or failure)
03The same probability of success, p, on every trial
04The trials are independent of each other
