Hypothesis Testing

A hypothesis test checks a claim about a population using a sample: we assume the claim is true and ask whether data like ours would be surprising. This page sets out the idea and the five steps, works through one example, lets you change the alternative hypothesis, the significance level and the sample mean to see how the decision changes, shows where the p-value comes from by simulation, and ends with a short quiz.

1

What is a hypothesis test?

A way to check a claim about a population (a mean, a proportion) using a sample. We assume the claim is true and ask: would data like ours be surprising?

1State two hypotheses

H₀, the null hypothesis: the claim we start from. It always has an equals sign.

HA, the alternative: what we look for evidence of.

H₀: μ = μ₀

2Compute a test statistic

How far is the sample mean from μ₀, in standard errors? If H₀ is true it follows a t-distribution with n − 1 degrees of freedom.

t = (ȳ − μ₀) / (s/√n)

3Ask how unusual it is

The p-value is the probability, if H₀ were true, of a test statistic at least as extreme as the one observed. Small p means strong evidence against H₀.

p ≤ α ⇒ reject H₀

Which alternative? It depends on the question

μ > μ₀Upper-tailed: is it higher than claimed? (pollution above the limit)
μ < μ₀Lower-tailed: is it lower than claimed? (vitamin C below the label)
μ ≠ μ₀Two-tailed: is it different in either direction? (a drug changes blood pressure)

Two ways to be wrong

H₀ is trueH₀ is false
Reject H₀Type I error (probability α)Correct
Do not reject H₀CorrectType II error

Drug trial: a Type I error approves a drug that does not work; a Type II error misses one that does.

The significance level α (usually 0.10, 0.05 or 0.01) is the Type I error risk we accept.E.g. α = 0.05 in the drug trial: if the drug really does not work, there is a 5% chance the trial still concludes it does. Test 100 useless drugs and about 5 would be approved by chance. It is not the chance that an approved drug is useless.t in rejection region ⇔ p ≤ α ⇒ reject H₀otherwise ⇒ do not reject H₀ (not the same as “H₀ is true”)
